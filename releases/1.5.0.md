New
- Customizable notch shape presets (`Classic`, `Liquid`, `Droplet`, `Flush`, `Custom`) with shoulder, corner radius, and taper sliders in Settings.
- Added the **Collapse idle notch to Mochi only** toggle in **Settings > Mochi** so you can disable the smallest Mochi-only state and always keep the full compact bar visible.
- **Mochi Overhaul**: bidirectional agent pipe (`MochiAgentPipeListener`) with auto-peek Approval/Question action pill, CLI-backed **MochiQuickAsk** overlay (including Antigravity CLI), unified right-click Wardrobe & Quick Controls menu, labeled mini-Mochi pills for parallel tasks, auto-fading desktop speech bubbles & status chip, **Hide notch while sleeping** and **Do Not Disturb** toggles, and `MochiQuotaTracker` (burn-rate ETA & quota reset celebration).

Improvements
- Streamlined BrimDeck to English-only UI, themes, and release notes; removed unused legacy device-battery, regional-player, and custom-JS-provider code.
- Lower idle and sleep CPU usage (~30 fps throttle, `FileSystemWatcher` for `.db*` activity detection).
- Upstream Antigravity and stability improvements: Argon weekly quota support, service-file port/token discovery, immediate collapse on outside click, and resilient startup/tray/audio handling.

Fixes
- Fixed duplicate Mochi click handling, hover-peek layout transitions, and Argon group inference in tests.
