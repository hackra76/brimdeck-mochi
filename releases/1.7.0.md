New Features
- **Nimbus Weather Reactions**: Nimbus now reacts to real-time local weather when idle or sleeping across 9 states (**Sunny**, **Rain**, **Thunderstorm**, **Snow**, **Windy**, **Fog**, **Heatwave**, **Clear Night**, and a 15-minute post-rain **Rainbow**), with custom body palettes, weather props, particles, auto-outfits (Sunglasses/Scarf, while preserving seasonal holiday outfits), and interactive poke Easter eggs. Real AI agent states always take priority.
- **Notch & Expanded Weather Forecast**: Added a compact weather badge (`CompactPart.Weather`) for the collapsed notch, capsule, and side-edge column, plus a new **Weather** page (`DeckPage.Weather`) in the expanded panel showing current conditions, Nimbus's weather quip, the next 6 hours, and a 5-day forecast.
- **Dedicated Weather Settings**: Added a **Weather** category in Settings for city search, one-shot **Detect** location, °C/°F unit selection, and display/reaction toggles, along with **Collapsed view** and **Expanded view** controls. Uses Open-Meteo (free, no API key required).
